Privacy Policy for Pinset

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Pinset organizes Chrome's native pinned tabs into named workspaces and restores the selected set in browser windows. This policy describes what the extension can access and what happens to that information.

What the extension accesses

To detect whether a configured pin is already open, Pinset reads tab URLs and titles inside your browser. This uses the tabs permission and happens when Chrome starts, a new window opens, or you use actions such as "Apply to this window" and "Import open pinned tabs".

What the extension stores

Pinset stores your pin URLs, labels and matching rules, workspace names, abbreviations, colors and membership/order, and extension settings in chrome.storage.sync. Chrome may sync this configuration across your signed-in browsers under your Google account. The active workspace selection and options-page presentation setting are stored locally in the browser profile with chrome.storage.local and do not sync. Chrome's sync is governed by Google's privacy policy.

What the extension does not do

Pinset does not send browsing data to the developer or to a developer-operated server. It has no analytics, tracking, or advertising and makes no network requests of its own. Chrome may sync the configuration above through the Chrome Sync service when enabled.

Data removal

Data is kept in Chrome extension storage. Removing the extension deletes its local data according to Chrome's extension lifecycle. You can also remove individual pins and workspaces from Pinset's options page.
